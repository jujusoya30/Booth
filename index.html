    About ♡

    Every Click Tells a Story. Photobooth is a free online photo booth that lives entirely in your browser. Pick a layout, take your photos with a countdown, decorate them with colors, stickers and doodles, then download a crisp strip that's ready to post.

    How it works

    1. Choose a layout: 2, 3 or 4‑cut strips, a 2×2 grid, a polaroid or a student ID card.
    2. Allow the camera (or upload photos).
    3. Pose during the countdown (10 seconds per shot, or pick 3 or 5).
    4. Decorate: frame colors, shapes, filters, stickers, doodles and captions.
    5. Download or share in the size you need.

    Add it to your home screen

    On Android or desktop Chrome/Edge, use the “Install app” button or the browser menu. On iPhone or iPad, tap the Share button in Safari, then “Add to Home Screen”.

    No accounts, no uploads, no watermark.

    Privacy Policy 🔒

    Your photos stay on your device.

    • Photos are captured and edited inside your browser. They are never uploaded to a server.
    • The camera is used only while you're on the camera page, and it turns off when you leave.
    • We don't use accounts, analytics or advertising trackers.
    • The only things saved in your browser's local storage are small preferences, like whether you've seen the welcome guide.
    • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a standard web request (no photos).
    • When you use Share, your image goes only to the app you choose on your device.
